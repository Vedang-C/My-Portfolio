Fitness UX Process

Problem Statement

Design a responsive fitness tracking web app that encourages daily engagement and simplifies progress visualization for users.

Design Thinking

Focused on understanding user goals, pain points, and motivations through journey mapping and persona development.

User Research

Conducted a survey and 1:1 interviews. Users wanted: quick logging, visual progress, mobile optimization, and intuitive dashboards.

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Wireframes included screens for workouts, calendar view, habit logging, and profile insights. Prioritized card layouts and visual indicators.

High-Fidelity Prototype

Designed in Figma with clean visuals, consistent spacing, and feedback animations. Ensured consistent styling for charts and stats.

Usability Testing

Tested with 6 users. Findings: calendar needed clearer labels, habit logging required fewer taps, and analytics were overwhelming.

Final Iterations

Simplified charts, added color-coded habits, refined navigation, and introduced tooltips for complex sections. Increased engagement.
