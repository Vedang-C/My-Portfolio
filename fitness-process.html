Fitness Website Application

Design Process Overview

🧩 Problem Statement

Many fitness platforms overwhelm users with too much data and unintuitive navigation. Our objective was to create a responsive, motivating dashboard that encourages users to stay consistent with their workouts.

🧠 Design Thinking Approach

We adopted a human-centered process with iterative feedback at each stage. Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test were central to our approach.

🔍 User Research

  • Surveyed 20 users who use digital fitness tools weekly
  • Top needs: progress tracking, simple goal setting, and positive reinforcement
  • Users disliked overly complex dashboards

📝 Low-Fidelity Wireframes

We mapped out a dashboard-centric interface with minimal cognitive load. The wireframes were refined after initial testing.

🎨 High-Fidelity Prototype

The UI used a dark/light toggle, clean typography, and gamified elements to keep users engaged.

View Final Prototype

✅ Usability Testing

  • 5 users tested main dashboard and workout flow
  • Found friction in weekly goal edit flow
  • Requested better calendar view of progress

🔄 Iterations Based on Feedback

Improved navigation clarity, added a weekly calendar view, and highlighted progress visually with animations and color-coded achievements.
