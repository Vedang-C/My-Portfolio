Food Delivery Platform

Design Process Overview

🧩 Problem Statement

Users often struggle with food delivery apps that are cluttered, hard to navigate, or inaccessible. The goal was to create a clean, mobile-first interface that prioritizes ease of use, quick ordering, and accessibility.

🧠 Design Thinking Approach

We followed a user-centered design process: Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test. Every stage aimed to keep the user’s experience at the center of all decisions.

🔍 User Research

  • Conducted 5 interviews with frequent food delivery users
  • Top frustrations: slow loading, confusing filters, limited accessibility options
  • Users prioritized speed, personalization, and real-time tracking

📝 Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Sketches and grayscale wireframes focused on layout, hierarchy, and simple navigation. We tested these early wireframes with users before moving to hi-fi designs.

🎨 High-Fidelity Prototype

The visual design followed a clean, accessible color palette with large touch targets and intuitive UX patterns.

View Final Prototype

✅ Usability Testing

  • Tested with 4 users using moderated remote sessions
  • All tasks completed with 100% success rate
  • Minor confusion around search bar placement and category labels

🔄 Iterations Based on Feedback

We refined the layout of the homepage, added clearer icons, and made category filters more prominent. A search autosuggest feature was also introduced.
