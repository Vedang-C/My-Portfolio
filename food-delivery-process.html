Food Delivery UX Process

Problem Statement

Design a mobile-first food delivery platform that offers fast, intuitive, and accessible ordering for busy urban users.

Design Thinking

We followed a human-centered approach: empathizing with users, defining pain points, ideating, prototyping, and testing.

User Research

Interviewed 8 users. Pain points included long checkout flows, unclear delivery estimates, and cluttered menus. Insights drove layout decisions.

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Sketched key screens: home, food category, product page, cart, and order tracking. Prioritized a frictionless flow from browsing to checkout.

High-Fidelity Prototype

Used Figma to create pixel-perfect mockups with brand colors, icons, and interactive components. Built a clickable prototype for testing.

Usability Testing

Tested with 5 participants. Noticed confusion around filter icons and multi-step checkout. Iterations made based on feedback.

Final Iterations

Streamlined checkout, added status progress bar, clarified navigation labels, and improved accessibility (contrast, button sizes).
